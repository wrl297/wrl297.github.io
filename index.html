I am Ronglong Wu, a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Informatics at Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Zhirong Shen and co-advised by Prof. Yiming Zhang. I received my B.S. degree from Xiamen University in 2021. From October 2025 to June 2026, I was a Research Intern in the Intelligent Scheduling Group (GPU) at Tencent.

My research interests lie in computer systems and architecture, with a particular focus on building efficient and reliable memory systems for emerging computing workloads, especially large language models (LLMs).
